What is a Word Counter?

A Word Counter is a free online tool that counts words, characters, sentences and paragraphs in your text. It can help students, writers, bloggers and professionals check the length of their writing.

How to use this tool

  1. Type or paste your text into the text box.
  2. View the word and character counts instantly.
  3. Click Copy Text to copy your text.
  4. Click Clear Text to start again.
